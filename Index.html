Our Inspiration

The Blackmar-Dimar Gambit

"Give material. Gain initiative. Create opportunities."

The Blackmar-Dimar Gambit is an ambitious chess opening beginning with 1.d4 d5 2.e4. White offers a pawn to develop quickly and seize attacking chances.
